Calculation aid only. Based on the Newborn Feeding calculation sheet and the PCMC maintenance table; check against your unit protocol and the baby's clinical state. GIR = % dextrose × rate (mL/h) ÷ (6 × weight kg). Target GIR: term 4–6, preterm 6–8 mg/kg/min. Rates are rounded up to the next 0.5 mL/h as in the sheet. Dextrose is counted at 3.4 kcal/g.
