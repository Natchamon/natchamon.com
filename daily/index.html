Calculation aid only. Milk is rounded to whole mL per feed and TV/TC come from the rounded amount; the lipid cut (50% at 80 mL/kg/d, off at 100) and protein/GIR notes follow the PCMC protocol. TPN doses, conversions (10% Aminoven g × 10, 50% dextrose g × 2, 3% NaCl mEq × 2, KCl mEq ÷ 2, K₂PO₄ mL = mEq, 10% Ca gluconate mmol × 4, 20% SMOF g × 5) and the final glucose formula come from the PCMC revised neonatal protocol and its parenteral nutrition order sheet. Osmolarity uses mOsm/mL per product from the unit's TPN calculator (50% dextrose 2.52, Aminoven 0.885, 3% NaCl 1.03, KCl 4, K₂HPO₄ 7.4, Ca gluconate 0.308, trace 0.1); limits are 900 peripheral and 1,200 central. Check against your pharmacy and the baby's labs before ordering.
