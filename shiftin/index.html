How it works

Two short paths that meet at a confirmed shift

Anyone can browse. Signing up and verifying is a short stop on the way, and the user always lands back on the shift that brought them in.

Principles

A neutral marketplace by design

ShiftIn connects people. It is not a healthcare provider, an agency, or an employer, and the product is built so it can't drift into being one.

ShiftIn never

  • Sets pay rates or schedules for doctors
  • Holds money, deposits or escrow
  • Takes a percentage of a doctor's pay
  • Charges anyone to get verified
  • Supervises clinical work

Safeguards built in

  • Licence-renting posts (แขวนป้าย) are rejected
  • Append-only audit log of every user action
  • Verification documents in private storage
  • Clinics accept responsibility each time they post
  • Self-service account deletion, blocked only while a shift is confirmed
