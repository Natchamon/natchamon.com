Calculation aid only. Percentiles are read from the curves of the Thai Society for Pediatric Endocrinology growth charts (2022): WHO Child Growth Standards 0–24 months (2006), WHO 2–5 years (2006) and the Thai National Growth References 5–19 years (Bureau of Nutrition, 2020). Curves were digitised monthly (P3, P10, P25, P50, P75, P90, P97) and the z-score and percentile are interpolated between them, so they are estimates (about ±0.1 cm or ±0.05 kg). The 0–24 month chart is used under 24 months, and the 2–19 year chart from 24 months. Chart images are the original charts.
