Calculation aid only. Ideal body weight = weight at P50 for the child's height on the Thai Society weight-for-height chart (2–19 years, 90–180 cm boys / 90–170 cm girls); the age formula is (age mo + 9) ÷ 2 for 3–12 months, age × 2 + 8 for 1–6 years, (age × 7 − 5) ÷ 2 for 7–12 years; % of IBW bands follow the Waterlow classification. BMI z-scores are read from the Thai Society BMI z-score charts (WHO 0–5 years; Thai National Growth References 5–19 years). Percentiles are read from the curves of the Thai Society for Pediatric Endocrinology growth charts (2022): WHO Child Growth Standards 0–24 months (2006), WHO 2–5 years (2006) and the Thai National Growth References 5–19 years (Bureau of Nutrition, 2020). Curves were digitised monthly (P3, P10, P25, P50, P75, P90, P97) and the z-score and percentile are interpolated between them, so they are estimates (about ±0.1 cm or ±0.05 kg). The 0–24 month chart is used under 24 months, and the 2–19 year chart from 24 months. Chart images are the original charts.
