Clinic & growth

Well-baby clinic Well baby clinic Tap-based visit record, 1 month to 5 years, with Thai growth charts and a Thai report for the chart. Growth Growth plotter Weight and length/height percentiles on the Thai pediatric endocrine charts, 0–19 y, with trends. IV fluids Paediatric IV fluids Holliday-Segar maintenance plus deficit replacement, loading dose and drip rate. Preterm Fenton plotter Weight, head circumference and length on the Fenton 2013 charts, with approximate percentiles.

Newborn & fluids

Milk Milk calculator Oral feeds by day of life and weight: volume per feed, calories and the order. Jaundice Neonatal jaundice Phototherapy and exchange thresholds by GA, age and TSB, with stopping criteria and follow-up. Newborn IV Neonatal IV & GIR IV rate for NPO babies, step feeds with IV, hypoglycaemia push and GIR check. Fluids & calories Neonatal TPN Milk advance by day, lipid cut, then TPN or D10: bag volumes, rates, GIR and order lines. Development Development (DSPM) Milestones by age band for all five domains: how to assess, pass criteria and how to train. Resuscitation Neonatal resuscitation ETT, blade, Neopuff, epinephrine, UVC depth and glucose by weight and GA (NCPR 2025). Screening Newborn screening Thyroid, hearing, CCHD, ROP, head ultrasound, osteopenia and anaemia of prematurity: what and when. Transfusion Exchange transfusion Total and partial exchange volumes by weight and Hct, with cycles and duration.
